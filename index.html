§ 01The problem

One title is easy to fix. Five hundred titles are a system.

Catalogs pick up inconsistencies over time. None of them is serious on its own. Together they turn into a review job nobody has scheduled: find every variation, decide which one is correct, and apply that decision everywhere without changing anything that should stay as it is.

  • Manual entry across seasons and staff
  • Multiple suppliers with their own naming conventions
  • Imported data that kept its original formatting
  • Merchandising changes applied to some listings and not others
  • Years of ordinary catalog maintenance
  • Brand - Productseparator
  • Brand Productno separator
  • Product by Brandbrand placement
  • PRODUCT NAMEcapitalization
  • Product Name - Colorattribute format
  • Product Name-Colorspacing

The problem is not the punctuation. The problem is deciding the standard and applying it consistently.

§ 02The service

We take ownership of the title review, not just the edits.

Small inconsistencies are easy to fix one at a time. Finding them across hundreds of listings, deciding the correct rule, applying it consistently, and knowing when not to change something is the actual work. That is what you are handing off.

  • We review

    We review the product titles in scope and identify repeated inconsistencies, formatting differences and naming patterns.

  • We define the standard

    Clear patterns are standardized. Decisions that depend on your business rules are surfaced for your input.

  • We apply it consistently

    Approved rules are applied across the titles in scope, the same way every time.

  • We verify

    The final result is reviewed so the work stays inside the approved title scope.

§ 04Flag, don’t guess

We don’t guess what your catalog means.

Some differences are clearly formatting. Others are business decisions. When two supplier names, brand-placement rules, abbreviations or internal codes appear in the catalog, Catalog Standard does not invent the answer.

We flag the decision once, get your direction, and apply the rule consistently. If it requires your judgment, we flag it instead of guessing.

§ 07FAQ

Questions merchants ask before starting.

Q1What exactly do you change?+

Product titles only. We review the titles in scope, propose a consistent naming standard, apply the rules you approve, and verify the result. Where a title needs a business decision, it is flagged for you rather than changed.

Q2Do you change prices, inventory or SKUs?+

No. Prices, inventory quantities, SKUs, barcodes, variants, variant structure, descriptions, SEO fields and image alt text are outside the editing scope of this service. Title scope means title scope.

Q3What happens if a title requires a business decision?+

It is flagged, not guessed. You see the question, choose the rule once, and we apply it consistently. Typical flags include brand placement, abbreviations, internal codes, and supplier naming that appears in more than one form.

Q4Do you need access to my Shopify store?+

Not always. The delivery workflow is confirmed in writing before work begins and depends on how you prefer to receive and apply the changes: a reviewed file for you to apply, or changes applied on your behalf under access you grant for the agreed scope.

Q5What if I have more than 500 products?+

The current service covers up to 500 active or prioritized product titles. You can tell us which titles matter most, or we can propose a selection for your approval before starting.

Q6How much does it cost?+

$390, one-time, for up to 500 product titles. There are no tiers, subscriptions or add-ons.

Q7How do I pay?+

Payment options include card and U.S. bank transfer. Payment details are confirmed by email before work begins.

Q8How long does it take?+

There is no fixed turnaround. The start date, delivery workflow and expected delivery date are confirmed in writing before work begins, based on catalog size, scope and how quickly flagged decisions are answered.
